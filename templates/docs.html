Project Overview & Problem Statement

Cocoa (Theobroma cacao L.) is an essential economic cash crop sustaining over 50 million smallholder livelihoods globally. However, annual yield destructions between 30% and 40% are caused by biological pathogens, predominantly Black Pod Rot (Phytophthora spp.) and the Cocoa Pod Borer (Conopomorpha cramerella). Traditional manual scouting is slow, subjective, and often misdiagnoses early infections, leading to complete crop failure.

Model Architecture & Transfer Learning

Stage 1: Cocoa Fruit Detector (Binary Classification)

Validates whether the image contains a legitimate cocoa fruit/pod (`cocoa_2.keras` / `cocoa_2.h5`). Uses pre-trained MobileNetV2 with GlobalAveragePooling2D, Dense(64, ReLU), Dropout(0.3), and Dense(1, Sigmoid). Trained with class weights to eliminate false positive non-agricultural uploads.

Stage 2: Multi-Class Disease Classifier (MobileNetV2 Transfer Learning)

Trained on 2,472 balanced images using on-the-fly augmentation (`rotation_range=20`, `zoom_range=0.15`, horizontal/vertical flips).

Cocoa Pathogens & Treatment Guide

Black Pod Rot (Phytophthora spp.)

Critical fungal pathogen causing water-soaked lesions and bean decay.

  • Chemical: Copper Hydroxide (2.5-3.0 g/L) every 14-21 days during rains.
  • Fertilizer: Potassium Sulfate (MOP) to thicken pod cuticle rinds.
  • Bio-control: Trichoderma harzianum / viride spray.
  • Cultural: Weekly sanitation pruning; deeply bury infected pods.
Cocoa Pod Borer (Conopomorpha cramerella)

Destructive moth larvae boring into rinds, causing clumped stunted beans.

  • Mechanical: Plastic sleeving/bagging of young pods (6-8 cm).
  • Insecticide: Cypermethrin or Chlorantraniliprole applied to rinds at dusk.
  • Fertilizer: Single Super Phosphate (TSP) and Sulfur conditioners.
  • Cultural: Complete 7-day harvesting to interrupt the moth life cycle.
Healthy Cocoa Pod (Theobroma cacao L.)

Optimal crop health with unblemished furrow ridges and high bean vigor.

  • Fertilizer: Balanced NPK 12-12-17+2MgO (350-500 g/tree/year).
  • Micronutrients: Foliar Zn, B, and Mg sprays during flowering.
  • Cultural: Maintain 30-40% canopy shade and clean weed-free tree basins.
Healthy Pod (Borer Resilient)

Resilient pod exhibiting natural thick cuticle resistance against boring pests.

  • Conditioner: Calcium and silicon soil conditioners.
  • Surveillance: Install sex pheromone sticky monitoring traps nearby.
  • Biological: Prophylactic neem extract kernel spray.
